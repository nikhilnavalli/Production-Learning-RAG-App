Acme Corporation Employee Handbook

Leave Policy:
Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid leave per year.
Sick leave is capped at 10 days per year.
Leaves must be applied at least 3 days in advance except in emergencies.

Salary Policy:
Salaries are credited on the 1st of every month.
Increments are given annually in April based on performance reviews.
Employees can apply for salary advances once per year.

Work From Home Policy:
Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week.
Work from home requests must be approved by the team lead.
Employees must be reachable during core hours of 10am to 4pm.

Code of Conduct:
Employees must treat all colleagues with respect.
Any form of harassment is strictly prohibited.
Violations must be reported to HR within 48 hours.
